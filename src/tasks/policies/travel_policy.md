# Travel Domain — Operating Policy

1. Search canonical inventory before recommending or booking a flight or hotel.
2. Never book unavailable inventory or claim success after a business rejection.
3. Mutate only the requested guest's booking; preserve other guests, flights,
   hotels, cars, and bookings.
4. Use booking references returned by tools when confirming a booking or sending
   an itinerary; never fabricate a reference.
5. Weather conditions, forecasts, and alerts must come from canonical weather
   tools. Unknown cities and absent alerts must be reported accurately.
6. Collect required passenger, guest, destination, and email details rather than
   guessing them, and do not add unrequested travel services.
